Purpose

  Compact navigation for the retained, current manuscript-rebuild repository.
  Historical, exploratory, superseded, and transient paths were relocated to
  junk/ with their original repository-relative layout. junk/ is local,
  Git-ignored, and not an active dependency.

Start here

  - docs/current_manuscript_rebuild.md defines the cleanup/rebuild boundary,
    validation commands, limitations, and recovery policy.

    manifest used by scripts/audit_cleanup_keep_roots.py.
  - AGENTS.md contains the HPC execution rules. In particular, invoke R scripts
    through scripts/agentRrunner.sh and use SLURM for jobs expected to exceed
    five minutes or require large/GPU resources.

    explicit and should not silently reactivate junk content.
