# Figure legends

## Figure 1. Cell and nuclear area across the complete available time courses

Median segmented-cell area (a) and nuclear area (b) for SUM-159-fuse C00,
SUM-159-fuse I00, SUM-159-chem M00b, and the GFP-classified mixture assay.
Columns contain the union of all G0 concentrations available across the four
contexts; blank panels denote combinations that were not acquired. Lines are
group medians and ribbons span the 10th–90th cell percentiles. Gold shading
marks 24–48 h, selected to reduce early seeding/attachment and late
confluence, starvation, and death effects. Stars identify 0.1, 0.5, and 1 mM
G0, the conditions available in all four contexts. Mixture low/high identity is
assigned by the GFP classifier and may be affected by classification error.

## Figure 2. Relationship between area phenotype and field confluence in the selected subset

Image-level median segmented-cell area (a,c) and nuclear area (b,d) plotted
against segmented-cell count (a,b) or total segmented area (c,d). Only the
24–48 h interval and the shared 0.1, 0.5, and 1 mM G0 conditions are included.
Each point represents an image×ploidy summary; lines are descriptive
within-group linear fits. The panels test whether the observed low/high area
ordering is explained solely by differing cell density or segmented coverage.
Partial separation of some low/high x-ranges limits causal interpretation.

## Figure 3. Area distributions and comparison of nominally identical 2N populations

Overlaid segmented-cell-area (a) and nuclear-area (b) distributions for
24–48 h at 0.1, 0.5, and 1 mM G0. Dashed vertical lines mark group medians.
Histograms are normalized within group and are not stacked. Low/2N median
segmented-cell area (c) and nuclear area (d) are shown relative to the chem 2N
median at the same G0; the dashed line at 1 indicates agreement with chem 2N.
Fuse and chem 2N are putatively the same cell line. The independently
classified mixture low population is included as a reference, but the mixture
classifier is not treated as an error-free gold standard.

## Figure 4. Cytoplasmic red reverses between fuse and chem after cell-size stratification

Image-level median cytoplasmic red after exclusion of a dilated nuclear region
and local-background normalization (a). Matched high-minus-low image-median
contrasts, where negative values indicate higher signal in low cells (b).
Matched high-minus-low contrasts after cells were divided into assay-specific
cell-area deciles and shared size strata were given equal weight (c). Points
represent matched field pairs or, for mixture, within-image comparisons; color
denotes G0. Seven representative 512×512-pixel fields at 1 mM G0 show phase,
mCherry/red, NIR, GFP where acquired, and a composite (d). Every segmented cell
boundary is displayed; cyan identifies low/2N and amber high/4N. GFP was not
acquired in the monoculture series and is explicitly marked. Red display limits
are shared across each paired low/high monoculture display. Size
standardization uses cell areas measured directly from the exact segmentation
masks used for the fluorescence calculation.
