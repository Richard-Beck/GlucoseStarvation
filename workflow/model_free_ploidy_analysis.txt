growth_lowG_median	Growth Low-G Median	Growth	Per condition, compute the smoothed maximum upward derivative of log1p(live_cells) over time, then take the median of those per-condition values across G0 <= 0.25 within each cell line and ploidy.	Higher values mean faster best-case live-cell expansion under very low glucose.
growth_highG_median	Growth High-G Median	Growth	Restrict to wells with G0 equal to 5 or 25. For each such well, fit log1p(live_cells) ~ hours from time 0 through the first timepoint where live_cells reaches 70% of that well's maximum observed live-cell count. The feature is the median fitted slope within each cell line and ploidy.	Higher values mean a faster estimated exponential-phase live-cell growth constant in high-glucose wells.
death_lowG_median	Death Low-G Median	Death	Per condition, compute the smoothed maximum upward derivative of log1p(dead_cells) over time, then take the median of those per-condition values across G0 <= 0.25 within each cell line and ploidy.	Higher values mean faster dead-cell accumulation under very low glucose.
death_highG_median	Death High-G Median	Death	Per condition, compute the smoothed maximum upward derivative of log1p(dead_cells) over time, then take the median of those per-condition values across G0 >= 1 within each cell line and ploidy.	Higher values mean faster dead-cell accumulation when glucose is not strongly limiting.
yield_alive_auc_intercept	Alive AUC Regression Intercept	Yield	For each condition, live_auc_glucose_window is the trapezoidal area under the live-cell trajectory between the first and last glucose measurement times. The feature is the intercept from regressing that quantity on log1p(G0), using only G0 <= 1.	Higher values mean higher baseline cumulative live-cell burden near zero glucose.
yield_alive_auc_slope	Alive AUC Regression Slope	Yield	For each condition, live_auc_glucose_window is the trapezoidal area under the live-cell trajectory between the first and last glucose measurement times. The feature is the slope from regressing that quantity on log1p(G0), using only G0 <= 1.	Higher values mean alive-cell burden rises more steeply as initial glucose increases.
peak_total_yield_intercept	Peak Total Yield Regression Intercept	Yield	For each condition, peak_total_yield_per_glucose is max(live_cells + dead_cells) up to the last glucose measurement, minus the initial total cell count at the first glucose measurement, divided by glucose_initial - glucose_final. Negative net gain is floored at zero before division, and the feature is the intercept from regressing that quantity on log1p(G0).	Higher values mean better baseline conversion of consumed glucose into total cell output near zero glucose.
peak_total_yield_slope	Peak Total Yield Regression Slope	Yield	For each condition, peak_total_yield_per_glucose is max(live_cells + dead_cells) up to the last glucose measurement, minus the initial total cell count at the first glucose measurement, divided by glucose_initial - glucose_final. Negative net gain is floored at zero before division, and the feature is the slope from regressing that quantity on log1p(G0).	Higher values mean glucose-to-total-cell conversion efficiency improves more strongly as initial glucose increases.
